carousel-container

scroll-snap-type

It takes two types: axis and a strictness.

Axis controls the scroll direction. Ex: x, y, or both

Strictness controls the behavior. Mandatory creates strict movement from one card to another, while proximity allows more flexibilty to scroll partway and then adjusts to the right card.

carousel-card

scroll-snap-align

This property controls the alignment of the snap target/card within the scroll container. It can be set to start, center, or end.

If you align the card to the start, it will snap to the left side of the container. Center will center it, and the end will snap to the right side of the container.

carousel-card

scroll-snap-stop

This determines whether a user can skip past snap points.

normal: The browser can skip over snap points during a scrolling gesture. always: The browser must stop at each snap point it passes during a scrolling gesture. This only works well if paired with mandatory snap type, because proximity is often inconsistent.

carousel-container

overflow-x, overflow-y

This determines what happens when content doesn't fit.

visible: Content extends beyond the container without being clipped.

hidden: Excess content is clipped, and the user cannot scroll to it.

auto: The browser provides scrolling when content overflows.

scroll: The container is scrollable, even when content doesn't overflow.

auto is usually the best choice, and it is what I have used for the carousel above.

carousel-container

overscroll-behavior

This determines what happens when a user scrolls beyond the carousel's boundaries.

auto: The browser uses its default scroll-chaining behavior.

contain: The browser prevents the scroll from chaining to an ancestor container.

Scroll chaining is basically when a user scrolls to the end of a container, and the browser continues scrolling the next section or down the page.

Summary

  • Try to think through the functionality of your carousel: is it meant to be linear (mandatory), or do the users need to be able to skip stop points (proximity)?
  • Use start, center, or end to control the alignment of the snap points.
  • Be aware of your content length and how it affects the scrolling behavior.
